Cameras 4 Kids

Donate a Working Camera

Any megapixel. Any brand. As long as it is a light capturing tool, it is good enough for a Light Writer.

A camera in a drawer makes no photographs. In the hands of a Light Writer, it will. Every working camera we receive goes to a student who is ready to learn.

Offer a Camera

We gladly accept

  • DSLR cameras
  • Mirrorless cameras
  • Film cameras
  • Point and shoot cameras
  • Lenses
  • Batteries and chargers
  • Memory cards
  • Tripods and camera bags

Financial support

Light Writerz is an official Pioneer Valley High School student club with its own club account. We are not accepting monetary donations online yet. If you would like to support the club financially, please contact us and we will follow up.
