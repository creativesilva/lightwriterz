Two of the four LWZ founders

Co-founder · Advisor

Chris Silva

Chris Silva is a fine art photographer and visual arts educator based in Santa Maria, California. He teaches Photography and Digital Arts at Pioneer Valley High School from Room 322. Working mostly in black and white, across film, digital, and hybrid workflows, he makes documentary work, environmental portraits, and experimental images rooted in place and lived experience.

His story with light began with a Nikon F3, “The Spirit Box,” placed in his hands by his uncle, a proud member of the Yaqui Nation. Chris studied Applied Arts and Design at Allan Hancock College and earned a BA in Photography from Southern New Hampshire University. He co-owns Cris & Laura Inspired EYE Images and believes a photograph should invite reflection and stand up to time.

Co-founder · Advisor

Laura Garcia

Laura Garcia is a professional photographer, photography educator, and co-founder of Light Writerz. Laura co-owns Cris & Laura Inspired EYE Images, bringing years of professional industry experience to the next generation of Light Writers.

As an LWZ advisor, Laura helps students build the discipline, technical skill, and creative confidence it takes to make photographs with intention.

The Founding Four

Est. 2010

In 2010, four professional photographers founded Light Writerz as a non-profit organization to inspire and educate young people in the true art of capturing light. Over the years LWZ grew into what it is today: a high school photography club at Pioneer Valley High School, led by students and carried by that same founding standard.
