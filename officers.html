The Founding Four

Founded 2010 · At PVHS since 2021

In 2010, four professional photographers founded Light Writerz as a non-profit organization to inspire and educate young people in the true art of capturing light. In 2021, just after students returned to classrooms following the COVID-19 pandemic, Mr. Silva brought LWZ to Pioneer Valley High School. Today it is a high school photography club, led by students and carried by that same founding standard.
