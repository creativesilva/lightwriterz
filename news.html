LWZ News

October 6, 2026 · Election

New officer position: Management Assistant

The Management Assistant supports the whole officer team, helping any officer in any capacity. One week that might mean helping the President run a meeting; the next, covering for the Vice President, helping the Secretary with attendance and notes, tracking supplies with the Treasurer, gathering photos for the PR / Media Officer, or setting up a project with the Creative Director.

It is a great fit for a member who is reliable, organized, and ready to pitch in wherever LWZ needs it. It is also the best seat in the club for learning how every officer role works.

Members vote at the LWZ meeting on Tuesday, October 13. Any member who has attended a meeting can submit a name: their own or another member's. Nominations close when that meeting starts.

October 6, 2026 · Club

Meeting check-in goes digital

Starting October 6, members check in to LWZ meetings by scanning a QR code with their phone and entering their student number.

Your first check-in builds your LWZ member profile. After that, checking in takes a few seconds.
