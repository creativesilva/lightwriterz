Who we are

Intention over volume

Light Writerz was founded in 2010 by four professional photographers: Chris Silva, Laura Garcia, Joshua Duffy, and Jeremy Wirth. Their goal was to inspire and educate young people in the true art of capturing light, at a highly creative and skilled level.

Today LWZ calls Pioneer Valley High School home. It is run by student officers and advised by two of its founders, Chris Silva and Laura Garcia. The logo carries pride and responsibility: to continue a legacy of image making at the highest level of intention and passion.

Why “Light Writerz”

We write with light

The word photography comes from Greek roots meaning “writing with light.” Every photograph is light, written onto a sensor or a piece of film. That is our name and our job: we are the Light Writerz.

The LWZ Shirt

Earned. Never given.

A Light Writerz shirt cannot be bought. It is only handed to students who have earned it by showing dedication and respect for the true art of making photographs.

If you see someone on campus wearing a Light Writerz shirt, you know they earned it. They are not a “picographer.” They are a photographer, dedicated to the art of making photographs.
